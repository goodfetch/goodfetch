## Notes

- C2: the arXiv abstract gives no numbers; the figures (two months, +30.3%, +41.6%, persistence) come from the HTML full text, section 4.1.2 and the introduction.
- C3: the paper's logo file is still named `evoclaw_icon.png`. The arXiv listing shows four versions (v1 13 Mar, v2 5 Jun, v3 10 Jul, v4 21 Jul 2026), and the rename happened after v1. "At most 38.03%" is the best model's score (Claude Opus 4.6).
- C4: the SWE-CI abstract does not mention the Maintainability Index; the claim comes from Observation 5 and Figure 8 ("Win/loss rate of LLM-generated code against human oracle code in terms of MI score (left) and Pylint score (right)"). The paper evaluates 20 models from 8 providers.
- C8: the same page also says that `CLAUDE.md` lines are followed "nearly every time" but "whether Claude follows that line on a given turn is still Claude's call."
- C11: the PDF is the authors' copy hosted on the last author's (Chunqiang Tang) site, with Meta Platforms affiliation and the SOSP '24 venue line (November 4–6, 2024, Austin). The abstract also states sensitivity "as small as 0.005%" and "around 800,000 time series".
- C15: the PDF's internal metadata names `wosp08-smith.dvi`, but the extracted text is titled "Software Performance Engineering Then and Now: A Position Paper" and is served from the ICPE 2015 WOSP-C proceedings path (DOI 10.1145/2693561.2693567 per secondary listing).
- C16: the file path claimed (`skills/bmad-prd/assets/prd-template.md`) exists on `main`. The older `src/bmm/...` paths return 404.
