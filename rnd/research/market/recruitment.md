# Recruitment for the interviews

**Status:** v1, ready to be published by the project owner. **Date:** 2026-10-09.
**Purpose:** find 10 to 15 people for the [interview guide](interview-guide.md) and the [pricing questionnaire](pricing-questionnaire.md), at least 3 per candidate segment of `D-segment`.

These messages are to be published or sent by the project owner personally, from their own accounts. The interviews cannot be simulated: an answer generated by a model never counts as evidence.

## 1. Channels

| Channel | Target segment | Format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Direct network (former colleagues, customers, developer friends) | all | Personal message, section 3 |
| LinkedIn, public post | `b2b-portals`, `data-teams` | Announcement, section 2 |
| r/webscraping, r/AI_Agents (rules of each subreddit to be checked before posting) | `agent-developers`, `data-teams` | Short announcement, section 2 |
| Discord servers of agent and scraping frameworks, "jobs" or "feedback" channel | `agent-developers` | Short announcement |
| E-commerce communities (Shopify, price monitoring) | `ecommerce-pricing` | Announcement, section 2 |

Only contact privately people who have opened that channel themselves, for example an address published to be contacted or a reply to the announcement. Do not gather contact details from several sources.

## 2. Public announcement

> **Do you automate websites? 30 minutes of your experience for a €50 gift card.**
>
> I'm building a web data and automation tool and I'm looking for people who, in the last year, switched tools or decided to build their own. That could be a scraping framework, a hosted browser, an AI agent or an access API.
>
> No sales pitch. I want to understand why you switched, what it costs and what breaks.
>
> 30–45 min video call, recorded only with your consent, anonymised transcript. DM me your role and the tool you use today.

## 3. Direct message (project owner's network)

> Hi [first name], I'm building a web automation tool and I'm doing a series of 30-minute interviews with people who collect data or automate websites. Have you or someone on your team switched tools recently, or built your own? No demo: I just want to understand how you do it today. Do you have a slot next week?

## 4. After a reply

1. Send the screening screen from the [guide](interview-guide.md), §1, in writing.
2. Give priority to people who have a dated switch to talk about, while respecting the quotas per segment.
3. Offer two slots. Recall the duration, the optional recording and the anonymization.
4. Record each candidate in the tracker below, without name or contact details: identifier `int-NN`, segment, status.
