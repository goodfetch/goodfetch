# Pitfall catalog

The [pain coverage matrix](pain-coverage.md) lists the pains people reported in issues and forums. This catalog lists the well-known failure modes of browser automation and crawling that experienced teams handle without filing issues, and says how Goodfetch closes each one. It was written by [Common pitfalls catalog](https://github.com/goodfetch/goodfetch/issues/547) on the map [#461](https://github.com/goodfetch/goodfetch/issues/461), from the [common pitfalls research](https://github.com/goodfetch/goodfetch/blob/research/common-pitfalls/rnd/research/2026-10-16-common-pitfalls.md) of 2026-10-16.

## How a row closes

- **Handled**: an adopted ADR on `main` ([`docs/adr/`](https://github.com/goodfetch/goodfetch/tree/main/docs/adr)) states the behaviour. The row also names what proves it:
  - a case of [`TESTING.md`](https://github.com/goodfetch/goodfetch/blob/main/TESTING.md), named by its bullet;
  - or a page of the Proving ground's list in ADR 0025 ("PG: …").
  Tests that an adopted ADR defines outside these two, such as ADR 0035's hermetic fixtures or ADR 0025's guard measurement, are cited beside them, never alone.
  ADR 0037's matrix rows are cited as `ADR 0037 / SA-24`. A value an ADR leaves to an area's spec is named as such. That does not make the behaviour undecided.
- **Handled, untested**: the behaviour is decided, but no `TESTING.md` case or Proving ground page proves it yet. The area's `/to-spec` gains that test as an acceptance criterion.
- **Gap**: no adopted decision states the behaviour. The row links the `wayfinder:grilling` ticket that decides it. Related gaps share one ticket.
- **Declined**: not answered, with the reason.

"Product" is where the behaviour lives: Collie, Foxhound (which contains Collie), or both. "Area" is the V1 area of ADR 0037 that specs it:
- 3 Engine runtime;
- 4 Security and egress;
- 5 Site access;
- 6 Collection;
- 8 Stealth and Challenge solving;
- 9 AI and agent;
- 11 Client surfaces;
- 13 Self-hosted deployment and operations.

**Sources.** "R 1.17" is item 17 of part 1 of the research, which lists every item's primary sources with full URLs. The row repeats the main one or two.
